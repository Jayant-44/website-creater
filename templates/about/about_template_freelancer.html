About Me

Hello, I'm Alex Chen.

I'm a freelance web developer and UX/UI designer based in Austin, TX. For the past six years, I've been helping startups and established businesses turn their complex problems into beautiful, simple, and intuitive digital solutions.

My design philosophy is simple: **put the user first**. I believe that great design is not just about aesthetics, but about creating an experience that feels effortless, accessible, and enjoyable for every user.

When I'm not coding or designing, you can find me hiking the greenbelt, trying out a new recipe, or getting lost in a good book.
