About Me

My name is Anya Kovacs. I capture moments in time.

I am a photographer and visual artist based in Berlin. My work explores the intersection of urban landscapes and human emotion, seeking to find the quiet, powerful stories hidden in plain sight.

Trained in analog film, my process is slow and deliberate. I believe in the power of a single image to convey a complex narrative. This website is a curated collection of my work, both personal and commercial.
