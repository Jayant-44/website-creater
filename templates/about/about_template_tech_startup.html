Our Story

QuantumAI was founded in 2021 by a small group of researchers who believed that the power of large language models shouldn't be locked away in the hands of a few tech giants.

We started with a simple open-source project. Today, we've grown into a global company, but our core mission remains the same: to build open, honest, and powerful AI that empowers human creativity and solves real-world problems.
