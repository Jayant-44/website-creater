Our Story

Founded on a simple idea, built with decades of passion. Follow our journey from a small workshop to a global leader in innovation.

2005: The Spark

Legacy Inc. was founded by Jane Smith in a small garage with a single product and a big dream: to make manufacturing more sustainable.

2010: First Milestone

We opened our first official factory, hired our 50th employee, and shipped our 10,000th unit to an international client.

2016: Innovation Award

Our patented "Eco-Weld" process won the National Innovation Award for reducing energy consumption by 40%.

2023: Global Expansion

We expanded our operations to three continents, serving over 50 countries and employing over 1,000 people worldwide.

2025 & Beyond

Our commitment to innovation continues as we invest in AI-driven logistics and fully recyclable materials, aiming for a carbon-neutral footprint by 2030.
