Our Philosophy

Meet Chef Isabella Rossi

Chef Isabella Rossi brings over 20 years of experience from the kitchens of Milan and New York. Her philosophy is built on the "farm-to-table" principle, a deep respect for seasonal ingredients, and the belief that a great meal is a celebration.

"For me, cooking is about memory," says Chef Isabella. "It's about the feeling of a shared meal, the taste of a perfectly ripe tomato in summer. That's what we try to capture at The Hearth—food that feels like home, only better."
